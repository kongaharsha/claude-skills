# Pricing Strategy

Help teams turn pricing signals, packaging information, project updates, and
customer-value evidence into clear pricing recommendations.

## Use When

- The user needs a competitor pricing scan.
- The user wants to compare packaging, tiers, value metrics, or bundles.
- The user needs a pricing risk and opportunity memo.
- The user wants to recap active pricing projects.
- The user is shaping pricing options, price increase messaging, or a value
  story.

## Working Principles

- Distinguish observed pricing facts from inferred pricing strategy.
- Capture source quality and timestamp for any pricing signal.
- Compare like-for-like where possible: product tier, packaging, geography,
  customer segment, and contract model.
- Surface implications for revenue, retention, competitiveness, sales motion,
  and customer value.
- Avoid overclaiming from public pricing when enterprise discounting or custom
  contracting may matter.

## Core Workflows

### Competitor Pricing Scan

Produce:

- competitor / product scope
- observed pricing and packaging
- recent pricing moves
- discounting or monetization signals
- risks and opportunities
- source table

### Packaging / Offer Comparison

Produce:

- feature / tier comparison
- packaging differences
- value metric assessment
- buyer implications
- open questions for Sales, Product, Finance, or Pricing

### Pricing Risk & Opportunity Memo

Produce:

- executive readout
- issue / opportunity
- evidence
- strategic implications
- options
- recommendation

### Pricing Project Recap

Produce:

- active projects
- objectives
- main achievements
- dependencies
- risks
- next milestones
- portfolio-level synergies

### Value Story Draft

Produce:

- value proposition
- economic rationale
- customer proof points
- objection handling
- sales / executive talking points

## Default Response Structure

1. Pricing answer
2. Evidence and source quality
3. Competitive / customer implications
4. Options
5. Recommendation
6. Follow-up questions
