# Stakeholder Map — Project Atlas
*Last updated: 2026-04-10*

## Why This File Exists

Different stakeholders care about different questions, levels of detail, and decision frames. This file helps tailor analysis, storyline development, and recommendations to the right audience.

## Core Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | What They Care About | What Good Output Looks Like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sarah | Executive sponsor | Big-picture risk, strategic choices, portfolio implications | Concise, decision-ready framing with clear choices and trade-offs |
| David | Strategy lead | Strategic logic, scenario framing, what matters over 12-36 months | Structured storyline, strong synthesis, explicit trade-offs |
| Maria | Business unit head | Practical implications for her P&L and confidence in the path forward | Sharp business framing, clear "so what," grounded recommendations |
| James | Product leadership | What this means for roadmap, build vs. buy, and execution timeline | Product-relevant implications, capability gaps, decision-support framing |
| Lisa | Finance leadership | Investment case rigor, downside protection, realistic assumptions | Crisp financial readout, downside-aware framing, no inflated projections |

## How To Use This

- Tailor the level of detail and framing to the audience
- Do not give every stakeholder the same style of answer
- When preparing a deck or analysis, think explicitly about who needs to be convinced and what question they are actually trying to answer
- When in doubt about tone or depth, ask which stakeholder this is for
