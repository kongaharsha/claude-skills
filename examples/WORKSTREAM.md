# Workstream: Market Sizing
*Last updated: 2026-04-10*

## Purpose

Define the addressable market for mid-market healthcare IT, identify the most attractive segments, and quantify the revenue opportunity under different penetration scenarios. This workstream feeds directly into the go/no-go investment case.

## Current Priorities

- [ ] Finalize TAM/SAM/SOM framework using bottom-up and top-down approaches
- [ ] Complete segment attractiveness scoring (growth rate, margin profile, competitive intensity, fit)
- [ ] Build penetration scenarios (conservative, base, aggressive) with revenue projections
- [x] Initial desk research on market size estimates from analyst reports

## Key Questions To Answer

1. What is the realistic addressable market — not the headline TAM, but the segment we could actually serve?
2. Which sub-segments are most attractive given our capabilities and positioning?
3. What penetration rate is realistic in year 1 vs. year 3, and what drives the difference?
4. How sensitive is the opportunity to pricing assumptions?
5. Are there segments where we'd be entering against entrenched incumbents vs. underserved gaps?

## Potential Outputs

- Segment attractiveness matrix (2x2 or scored ranking)
- TAM → SAM → SOM waterfall with assumptions documented
- Revenue scenario model (3 scenarios, 3-year horizon)
- One-page summary for the steering committee deck

## Findings

### Market size estimates (initial)
- Total healthcare IT market: ~$280B globally, growing at ~12% CAGR
- Mid-market segment (200-2,000 beds): estimated $35-45B, growing faster at ~18% CAGR
- Source: Gartner 2025 report + internal analysis of CMS hospital data
- **Caveat:** these are top-down numbers. Bottom-up sizing not yet complete.

### Segment attractiveness — early read
- Ambulatory care IT looks most attractive: high growth, fragmented competition, good fit with our platform capabilities
- Inpatient EHR-adjacent tools: large but dominated by Epic/Cerner ecosystem, harder to enter
- Revenue cycle management: attractive margins but commoditizing fast

### Open finding
- Need to validate whether mid-market buyers actually have budget authority or if purchasing rolls up to health system level. This changes the go-to-market significantly.
