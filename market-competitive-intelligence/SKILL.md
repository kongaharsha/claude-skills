# Market / Competitive Intelligence

Help teams monitor markets, competitors, customers, vendors, and strategic
signals in a repeatable way.

## Use When

- The user needs a weekly competitor recap.
- The user wants a market landscape scan.
- The user needs a competitor, vendor, or market profile.
- The user wants to monitor M&A activity, funding, partnerships, launches, or
  leadership moves.
- The user needs market sizing inputs or a first-pass market map.

## Working Principles

- Include source dates and source quality when available.
- Separate market facts, competitor actions, interpretation, and implications.
- Prioritize signals that could change strategic judgment.
- Do not overstate weak or single-source signals.
- Close with "so what" implications and follow-up questions.

## Core Workflows

### Weekly Competitor Recap

Inputs:

- competitor list
- time period
- markets or topics of interest

Produce:

- key news
- product / technology updates
- commercial or operating signals
- leadership or hiring changes
- funding, investment, or M&A activity
- notable quotes
- strategic implications
- source table

### Market Landscape Scan

Produce:

- market definition
- key players
- customer segments
- growth drivers
- trends
- risks
- competitor movement
- strategic implications

### Competitor / Market Profile

Produce:

- business summary
- product and technology details
- customer segments
- operating model
- positioning
- differentiation
- known weaknesses
- open questions

### M&A Activity Monitor

Produce:

- relevant deals
- buyer / seller / target
- strategic rationale
- valuation signals if available
- market implications

### Vendor Identification

Produce:

- vendor universe
- segmentation
- profiles / strips
- fit screen
- short list and rationale

## Default Response Structure

1. Key takeaways
2. Evidence
3. Strategic implications
4. Risks / uncertainties
5. Follow-up questions
