# Competitive Landscape
*Last updated: 2026-04-10*

## Why This File Exists

This is a threat model, not a fact dump. It captures the structural competitive dynamics that shape our entry strategy and should be updated as new intelligence emerges.

## What Is Changing

- Mid-market healthcare IT has shifted from a "nice to have" to a compliance and efficiency necessity, pulling in new entrants
- AI-native startups are compressing the time-to-market for new products, lowering barriers to entry
- Platform consolidation (Epic, Oracle Health) is creating ecosystem lock-in that shapes distribution options
- Buyer expectations are shifting from point solutions to integrated platforms

## Key Competitors

| Player | Position | Strength | Vulnerability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incumbent A | Dominant in enterprise, expanding down-market | Brand, install base, ecosystem | Slow to innovate, pricing pressure from below |
| Startup B | AI-native, fast-growing in ambulatory | Speed, UX, modern architecture | Unproven at scale, limited enterprise relationships |
| Startup C | Vertical SaaS for revenue cycle | Deep domain, strong NPS | Narrow scope, vulnerable to platform players |
| Platform D | Horizontal platform adding healthcare vertical | Distribution, capital, cross-sell | Shallow domain expertise, trust gap with clinicians |

## Implications For Our Entry

- **Ambulatory is the most open segment** — enterprise is locked up by incumbents, but mid-market ambulatory is fragmented and underserved
- **Speed matters more than completeness** — AI-native startups are moving fast; a slow, feature-complete launch will arrive too late
- **Partnership could leapfrog build timeline** — several mid-market players would benefit from our distribution and data assets
- **Trust is a differentiator** — in healthcare, credibility and compliance track record matter more than in most B2B markets

## What To Monitor

- Incumbent A's mid-market pricing moves (any aggressive discounting signals they see the same opportunity)
- Startup B's next funding round and enterprise deals (signals whether they can scale)
- Epic's app marketplace expansion (could open or close distribution paths)
- Regulatory changes around AI in clinical settings (could raise barriers to entry for all players)
